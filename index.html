A twelve-minute game you play by breathing. It starts with sips, builds a town out of box breaths, then launches you through three Wim Hof rounds.

The camera watches your chest rise and fall so the game can react to you. Nothing is recorded and nothing leaves this device. Without it, hold the space bar to breathe in.

Before you start

  • Sit or lie down somewhere safe.
  • Never do this in water, in the bath or while driving.
  • Skip the breath holds if you are pregnant or have epilepsy, a heart condition or high blood pressure.
  • Tingling and light-headedness are normal. If you feel faint, stop and breathe normally.
