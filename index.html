Ancient breathing, new game. Play it with care.

These are serious breathing techniques. They can make you light-headed and, rarely, faint.

  • Sit or lie down. Never force it.
  • Never in or near water, while driving or operating machinery.
  • Not if you are pregnant or have epilepsy.
  • Heart, blood-pressure or other medical condition? Ask a doctor first.
  • Tingling is normal. Feeling faint? Stop and breathe normally.
