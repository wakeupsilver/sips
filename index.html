Sip air to win, a 12 minute breathing game

It starts with a glide on small sips of air, builds a town out of box breaths, then launches you through three Wim Hof rounds.

Camera setup: sit so the camera sees your whole face and your upper chest, then stay fairly still.stand your phone on a table, leaning on something, about an arm's length away, so it sees your whole face and your upper chest. Don't hold it in your hand. The game then reacts to your chest rising and falling.

Nothing is recorded and nothing leaves this device. Without the camera, just follow along. If you hear nothing, switch off silent mode.

Ancient breathing, new game. Play it with care.

This is a serious breathing-technique adventure. The exercises are strong: they can make you light-headed and, rarely, faint.

  • Sit or lie down somewhere safe, and never force it.
  • Never play in or near water (sea, pool, bath or shower), while driving or operating machinery, or anywhere fainting could hurt you.
  • Do not play if you are pregnant or have epilepsy.
  • If you have heart or blood-pressure problems, or any other medical condition, do not play without asking a doctor first.
  • Tingling and light-headedness are normal and pass. If you feel faint, stop and breathe normally.
  • This is a game, not medical advice.
