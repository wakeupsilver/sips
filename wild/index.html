This one is not relaxing. Play it with care.

The levels throw fast, odd breathing at you. Fast breathing can make you light-headed.

  • Sit down. Rest between levels; the game makes you.
  • Dizzy, tingly or short of breath? Stop and breathe normally.
  • Never in or near water, while driving or operating machinery.
  • Not if you are pregnant or have epilepsy. Heart, lung or blood-pressure condition? Skip this one.
  • Some levels flash and shake the screen.

A game, not medical advice.
